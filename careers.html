Technical Academic Content

Technical Academic Content Writer

Newtown, Kolkata (On-site) Full-time Fresher (0–1 year) 4 vacancies 1-month probation

Research technical topics and write original, well-referenced academic content in programming, databases, data analysis, machine learning, networking and software development.

Key responsibilities

  • Research topics using credible academic sources and write clear, well-structured technical content.
  • Write and edit study guides, research articles, reports and technical documentation to academic standards.
  • Produce 100% original, human-written content that passes plagiarism and AI-detection checks.
  • Apply citation styles correctly (APA, MLA, Harvard and others).
  • Work with editors and subject matter experts to improve content quality.
  • Keep writing style, formatting and tone consistent with project requirements.
  • Manage multiple assignments and meet deadlines.

Required skills

  • Excellent written English and research skills.
  • Working knowledge of at least some of: databases, frontend and backend development, Java, machine learning.
  • Understanding of academic writing conventions and referencing.
  • Ability to explain complex technical topics clearly.
  • Proficiency in MS Word, Google Docs, MS Excel, MS PowerPoint and referencing tools.

Eligibility

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree, preferably in IT, CSE, BCA or a related field.
  • Own laptop or computer and a reliable home internet connection.
Marketing

Trainee Marketing Manager

Newtown, Kolkata (On-site) Full-time Fresher (0–1 year) 1 vacancy 1-month probation

Promote our services, generate and follow up on leads, and build Excedence’s presence among students, colleges and research scholars. You will be trained on our services and work closely with the founders and the content team.

Key responsibilities

  • Generate leads for our academic content, research support and training services through social media, WhatsApp, email and direct outreach.
  • Respond to inquiries professionally and convert them into clients through follow-ups.
  • Plan and run online campaigns on platforms such as Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook and WhatsApp.
  • Coordinate with the content team to understand service capabilities and communicate them accurately to clients.
  • Research the market, competitors and student needs, and suggest new ways to reach customers.
  • Reach out to colleges, institutions and student communities to build partnerships.
  • Maintain a lead and client tracker and prepare regular reports on enquiries, conversions and campaign results.
  • Meet monthly targets and handle multiple tasks within deadlines.

Required skills

  • Excellent verbal and written communication in English (Bengali and Hindi are a plus).
  • Confident, persuasive and comfortable talking to new people over calls and messages.
  • Basic understanding of digital marketing and social media.
  • Proficiency in MS Word, MS Excel, MS PowerPoint and Google Workspace. Familiarity with Canva or similar design tools is an advantage.
  • Willingness to learn, take feedback and work toward targets.

Eligibility

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in any field (BBA, MBA, Marketing, Mass Communication, BCA, IT or similar preferred).
  • Own laptop or computer and a reliable home internet connection.
